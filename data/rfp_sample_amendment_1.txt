HARBOR COUNTY TRANSIT AUTHORITY
RFP 2026-11 — CONTRACT MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
AMENDMENT NO. 1 — Issued September 25, 2026
This Amendment modifies RFP 2026-11. All other terms remain in force.
A1.1 Section 3.2 (Key Dates) is revised: the Proposal Submission
Deadline is extended from October 16, 2026 to OCTOBER 30, 2026,
2:00 PM PT. All other dates in Section 3.2 are unchanged.
A1.2 Section 7.1 (Bid Deposit) is revised: the bid deposit is
increased from $20,000 to $35,000.
A1.3 Section 5 (Eligibility) is supplemented by new Section 5.3: each
Respondent shall complete the security questionnaire in Appendix G
(issued with this Amendment) and include it with its proposal.
