HARBOR COUNTY TRANSIT AUTHORITY
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS RFP 2026-11
CONTRACT MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
Issued: August 3, 2026 | Fictional sample document written for this work sample
================================================================
SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION (pp. 1–2)
================================================================
1.1 Purpose. Harbor County Transit Authority ("the Authority") seeks
proposals for a hosted contract management platform to store, search
and track obligations in approximately 4,000 active vendor contracts.
1.2 Term. The initial contract term is three years, with two optional
one-year renewals at the Authority's sole discretion.
================================================================
SECTION 3 — SCHEDULE (pp. 3–4)
================================================================
3.1 Questions. Written questions must be submitted through the
procurement portal no later than September 18, 2026.
3.2 Key Dates. Proposal Submission Deadline: October 16, 2026, 2:00 PM
PT. Shortlist notification: November 20, 2026 (estimated). Contract
award: January 2027 (estimated).
3.3 Late Proposals. Proposals received after the deadline will not be
opened. The portal timestamp is final.
================================================================
SECTION 5 — ELIGIBILITY (pp. 5–6)
================================================================
5.1 Experience. Each Respondent shall demonstrate at least two
deployments of a contract management platform for public agencies.
5.2 Data Residency. All Authority data shall be stored in data centres
located in the United States.
================================================================
SECTION 7 — FINANCIAL SECURITY (pp. 7–8)
================================================================
7.1 Bid Deposit. Each Respondent shall post a bid deposit of $20,000
in the form of a cashier's check or bid bond with its proposal.
7.2 Return of Deposit. Bid deposits of unsuccessful Respondents will be
returned within 30 days after contract award.
================================================================
SECTION 8 — EVALUATION (pp. 9–10)
================================================================
8.1 Evaluation Weights. Proposals will be scored as follows: Technical
fit 40%; Security and compliance 25%; Price 25%; References 10%.
8.2 Interviews. The Authority may invite shortlisted Respondents to a
demonstration. Demonstrations are not separately scored.
